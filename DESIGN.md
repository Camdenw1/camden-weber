# Camden Weber website design brief

Global reference: [Personal Web Design System](</Users/camdenweber/Documents/Second Brain/Design/Personal Web Design System.md>) (2026-09-29). Read it alongside this brief for future design work.

## Identity
A personal home for Camden's work, writing, studies, and outdoor life. Visitors should easily explore those interests and read articles. Calm, approachable, polished, with real personal photographs. Utility/art balance: 5; balanced density. Preserve Camden's conversational copy and confirmed facts.

## Visual direction
- White base (#FFFFFF), neutral charcoal (#292929), muted gray (#646464), light gray borders. Keep the existing system dark theme with equivalent readable roles.
- Primary accent: rustic orange (#AA532F) for filled actions with fixed light text. Soft clay (#D6B09B) fills Recreation signs and bib bands with charcoal text; deeper clay (#875139) for links. Sage remains only a quiet supporting tint, not a prominent button or card color. This project palette is Camden's explicit preference (2026-09-29).
- Lora for sturdy serif headings; DM Sans for body and navigation; Recreation alone uses Barlow Condensed for bib numbers and times. Body 16–17px, comfortable line spacing, article width about 65 characters.
- Rounded photos (16–24px), controls (10px), small thumbnails (12px). Quiet borders; no ornamental shadows or glass.
- Homepage: clear introduction beside a candid outdoor photograph, followed by three open photo-led sections. One filled resume action, one project link. Stack in reading order on mobile.
- Writing: clear rows, dates and reading time, rounded thumbnails; article photography respects the original aspect ratio. No blurred duplicate photo backgrounds.
- Motif: topographic lines only on Recreation. Preserve its user-approved trail signs, trail markers, and race bibs, with lighter accent fills.
- Motion: brief hover feedback only; immediate content; reduced-motion support.

## Structure and behavior
Keep Home, About, Resume, Recreation, and Writing navigation. Projects remain accessible through Resume; retain study links and contextual article navigation. Current location, keyboard focus, mobile menu, and 44px principal controls remain clear. Keep existing empty states and map loading placeholder. Do not invent interactive features or course takeaways.

## Project exceptions
Recreation retains more decoration because Camden explicitly likes its outdoor identity. Use rounded bibs and clay trail signs without removing those details. Await better photo originals; use existing assets without artificial enhancement. No article copy edits as part of visual work.

## Review
Check home, writing, an article, About, and Recreation on desktop and narrow mobile; verify contrast, subject cropping, overflow, keyboard navigation, and production build. Show the local preview before pushing this revision.

San Diego is an important part of Camden's identity. Explore a subtle coastal detail in a later design discussion; no new decoration is agreed yet.
