## History

Nobody looks forward to Tuesday. Tuesday is the most boring day of the week. But there are eight of us who spend the other six days of the week looking forward to it.

Every Tuesday, after work, we have a recreational men's league basketball game.

It all started nine years ago at Hilltop Park. Back then, Saturday mornings were reserved for basketball. We would play for three hours, game after game, and end it with a group food outing, usually at our local bagel shop.

Everyone has their own unique game that they've built based on their skills, athleticism, and basketball IQ. Personally, I started as a defensive hustle player and slowly developed my shooting and guard skills to become a solid all-around guard for the team. Grant had a deadly midrange; Jake played like Angel Reese, missing shots in places only he could get the rebound; Jack R became skilled at driving to the basket using clever footwork.

As we started to learn each other's skills, we built up chemistry. We were experts of each other's games. Building basketball chemistry is very similar to building group friendships. You start to learn how to work with each other, what flies, what doesn't, who wants to start the possessions, and who should defend the best opponent. Our roles became unspoken, similar to how it is in the friend group.

Then college happened and everyone left. I went to UCLA, Grant went to Chapman, Cody went to Colorado State. Others went to UCSB, Cal Poly, Utah. Our rhythm was disrupted but our chemistry never died. Christmas and summer breaks were a great opportunity to reignite the basketball flame, and time after time, it did. It was truly the one thing we could count on to get everyone together.

Fast forward to post-grad. A few of us are back home. We decided to give the men's recreational basketball league a shot. We reached out to many people, and the group that ended up sticking after three seasons wound up being the same guys who started: my close friends and the basketball team.

Almost 20 players have come through our team over the years, and the most dedicated ones were the ones who stayed. It takes a certain shared passion to block off every Tuesday for basketball, along with the fee. That shared commitment is what makes it so special.

When we come together on Tuesdays, we are excited to work together and win games. Our team right now is beautifully constructed.

## The Roster

Jack B is our ball-dominant scorer. He can shoot, get to the rim, and always makes the best decisions. If we need a bucket, the ball should probably be in his hands. He was my best friend in elementary school, and it has been awesome to reconnect with him.

Grant is the next most ball-dominant guy. He is great at making the defense move and getting our offense started. His basketball career was born in passion and it shows. He's always determined to win and never thinks a defensive assignment is too much. On top of this, he is getting his career started down the CFP route. He shares his personal finance knowledge with the team constantly and we all appreciate it.

I round out the guard group. I thrive on consistent hustle and hard work. I am a streaky three-level scorer who has developed since we started. I also enjoy finding sneaky passes to get assists. I am also the GM of the team, handling league fees, roster decisions, and keeping the team informed.

Krishna is a scorer. He gets the defense where he wants them and isn't afraid to take them to the rim. He can also shoot well. He works well in isolation. Krishna worked in New York for the NFL for a while and joined us midway through.

Travis is the pinnacle of 3-and-D players in the league. He has single-handedly destroyed opposing offenses, getting more steals in a game than I knew was possible. On top of this, he is a sharpshooter on offense, often leading our team in points. I have never seen a player get so hot during a game.

Andrew is the first of our three big men. Andrew's top three skills are passing, rebounding, and finishing. Recently, he has also added a three-ball to his game, which always gets the team hyped up. His imitations of LeBron have definitely helped him become the passer he is today. Andrew is also Mr. Daygo.

Luke is Mr. Fundamental. A strong, smart, and reliable player who can defend the toughest opponents, facilitate the offense as a passer, and keep the ball in our possession. He doesn't need the ball in his hands to have a massive impact on the game. Luke also played college baseball. He is one skilled man.

Lastly, we have Adam. Adam is our tallest player, coming in at 6'5". For his height, he has great ball handling and shooting. He is most valuable as a rim protector and a board magnet. He gets many blocks, rebounds, and put-backs. Adam just got back from a couple-month-long trip to Southeast Asia, where he surely got more comfortable as the big.

## The Outcome

Last season we got to the championship game. We had many people come: parents, friends, girlfriends, and siblings. We were so locked in and determined. I have known these guys for years, and this is the most serious I have ever seen them. This mattered to us.

Everyone played their game and we won our first championship of many.

Celebrating wins with this team is always a blast. We usually go to In-N-Out, and because of that, I plan on getting a signed In-N-Out hat and framing it because of what this team means to me.

A greater lesson from this is the benefit of having scheduled productive activities with people you like. It is the single best way to become closer with people, and the consistency ensures progress in your skills.

I am now looking to establish more activities like this. The next one on the docket is a book club with my college friends. The goal is to read more, connect more, and enjoy scheduled conversation with those close to me.
